What I do

Haptics 🫳🏼🫆 Understanding and leveraging human perception to design haptic interfaces and interaction techniques to enhance touch-based experiences.

User Studies 🧑🏼‍💻💁🏼 Developing experimental protocols to assess user perception, experience, and behavior in response to multisensory, immersive, interactive systems.
